Opportunity Should Reach Every Learner.

A student’s future can be shaped by more than classroom learning. Access to guidance, resources, encouragement and real-world experiences can help young people see new possibilities for themselves. STG works with students, schools, families, volunteers and partners to make these opportunities more accessible, and to support progress from foundational learning to future pathways.

One Journey. Five Stages of Growth.

The Kalpavruksha 5G Education Model connects the stages of a student’s development into one continuous journey. Each stage builds on the previous one, helping learners move from discovering their strengths to applying their skills and contributing to their communities.

Empower

Seed

Discover potential through student assessment, aptitude and interest exploration, talent mapping and scholarship identification.

Engage

Root

Build strong foundations through academic support, mentoring, parent connection and foundational digital skills.

Enact

Branch

Learn by doing through STEM activities, projects, competitions, workshops and exposure experiences.

Enrich

Tree

Develop future-ready capabilities through career guidance, communication, leadership, digital and AI awareness, life skills and wellbeing.

Excel

Fruit

Progress towards scholarships, higher education, career opportunities, leadership and alumni mentoring.

Grounded in Need. Designed for Progress.

STG identifies student, school and community needs, assesses priorities, designs practical interventions, delivers them with local partners, tracks progress and improves through feedback.

1

Listen

Understand the needs of students, schools and communities.

2

Assess

Identify learning gaps, interests and priorities.

3

Plan

Match each need to the right stage and activity.

4

Deliver

Work with educators, mentors, volunteers and partners.

5

Track

Monitor participation, progress and opportunities accessed.

6

Improve

Learn from feedback and strengthen future delivery.
