EMPOWER — SEED | SCHOLARSHIP READINESS

Helping Students Prepare for the NMMS Scholarship Opportunity.

The NMMS Success Program helps students and families understand the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) opportunity and supports learners through structured preparation, practice and guidance. The aim is to make the process clearer and help students approach the examination with stronger preparation and confidence.

Preparation, Practice and Guidance, Step by Step.

For students, a scholarship examination can feel unfamiliar without clear information or regular practice. STG brings together awareness, learning support and assessment activities to help students understand what to expect and identify where they need more preparation.

Eligible students and their families. Ask us about the current criteria.

Where STG works

How it runs

Classes in person or online, with regular practice and mock tests, where offered.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NMMS Success Program?

It is STG’s program to help students and families understand the NMMS scholarship opportunity and access preparation support and practice activities.

Does joining the program guarantee a scholarship?

No. Participation does not guarantee selection. Scholarship awards are determined through the official examination and applicable rules.

What activities may be included?

Depending on the current schedule, activities may include awareness sessions, tuition or online classes, practice exercises, mock tests and guidance.

How can a school or volunteer get involved?

Contact STG to discuss student awareness, preparation support, teaching, mentoring or other relevant contributions.

Where can families check eligibility and application rules?

Families should consult current official NMMS and relevant state education department notifications. STG can help direct families to verified information.
